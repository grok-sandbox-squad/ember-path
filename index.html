How to play
  • You and the computer share one spark (◆) on a 6 by 6 board of coals worth 1 to 5 points.
  • The second player picks where the spark starts: any cell. That cell burns out and scores nothing. The second player begins with 1.5 points; there's no end bonus.
  • Then take turns moving the spark 1, 2 or 3 cells in a straight line: up, down, left or right.
  • The spark can't jump over or land on a burned-out cell (×), and it can't leave the board.
  • You score the number on the cell where the spark lands, then that cell burns out.
  • When the player to move has no move left, the game ends. Highest score wins; the half point means there are no draws.
  • On your turn, the cells you can reach are outlined: tap one.
